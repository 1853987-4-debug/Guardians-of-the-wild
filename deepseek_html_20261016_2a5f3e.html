Guardians of the Wild

Tranquilizer Patrol · Save the Pangolins!

How to Play

Goal: Tranquilize the poachers before they grab a pangolin. Don't shoot the pangolins!

  • 🖱️ Aim: Move mouse / finger
  • 🔫 Shoot: Click / tap to fire
  • 💤 Tranquilize: 3 darts = asleep
  • 🪙 Reward: Each defeated poacher gives +5 coins
  • 🦔 Don't shoot pangolins: You lose a life!
  • 🎯 Combo: Hit poachers in a row for bonus
  • ⚡ Crates: Shoot for rapid-fire / slow-mo / +life
  • 🛒 Shop: Press E (or tap 🛒) to buy fences
  • 🚧 Fence: Poachers attack fences first! They must break the fence before they can reach pangolins.
  • ⏱️ Lifetime: Cheaper fences last less time. Wooden = 8s, Steel = 14s, Spike = 22s
  • 🔄 Rotate: While placing, press ↑ ↓ ← → to rotate the fence
  • 📚 Quiz: A wildlife quiz appears every ~15s. Answer correctly for +1 coin! Game pauses while you answer.

Win: Survive 5 waves with pangolins alive.

Pro tip: Pangolins are the most trafficked mammals in the world. Their scales are sadly prized. Save them!
